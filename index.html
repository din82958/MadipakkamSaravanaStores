Our legacy

Trusted by Chennai families since 1980

Madipakkam Saravana Stores began in Madipakkam in 1980, bringing families vessels, kitchenware, pooja items and plastic household goods at fair prices.

From a mother's seer vessels to her daughter's first kitchen – families have come back to us for generations. In 2023 we opened our new, larger store in Medavakkam.

Frequently asked questions

Something else? Call or WhatsApp either store.

Do you offer free home delivery?

Yes. We deliver free on the same day to Medavakkam, Madipakkam, Pallikaranai, Velachery, Sholinganallur, Perumbakkam and nearby areas. Order in store, by phone or on WhatsApp.

Can you handle bulk orders for weddings and functions?

Yes. Bulk orders of any quantity – for weddings, seer varisai, housewarmings, return gifts, festivals and offices – are prepared and delivered in one day, at our everyday low prices.

Where are your stores?

Madipakkam: Shop No. 2, Medavakkam Main Road, near Pathala Vinayagar Kovil, Madipakkam, Chennai – 600091 (96984 71616). Medavakkam: 2/2H, Medavakkam Main Road, near Anjaneyar Kovil, United Colony, BHEL Nagar, Medavakkam, Chennai – 600100 (94445 77336).

What are your opening hours?

Both stores are open from 10:30 AM to 9:30 PM.

What do you sell?

Stainless steel and brass vessels, dinner sets, tiffin boxes, pressure cookers, idli cookers, kadai and tawa, gas stoves, mixer and wet grinders, flasks, plastic buckets, chairs and containers, brass vilakku, pooja items, seer vessels and return gifts.

Do you repair mixies and wet grinders?

Yes. Drop your mixie or wet grinder at our store – it is usually ready in 1–2 days, at honest, affordable charges.

How long have you been in business?

Madipakkam Saravana Stores has served Chennai families since 1980. Our first store is in Madipakkam, and we opened our second, larger store in Medavakkam in 2023.
